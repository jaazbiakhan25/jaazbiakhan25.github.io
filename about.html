About me

I didn't start out in UX.I started with people.

I am a product designer, but my inspiration behind design has always been people.

While for me art is a passion, over the course of my career I've used it as a force for good.

As far back as I can remember, I've always played with colors, patterns, and designs. I've played with Babushka dolls, painted street murals, tinkered with designs, crocheted blankets, and tried my hand at pottery.

In a truly millennial fashion, I've worked in IT, advocated for vulnerable folks through an NGO, and led art classes. My path has taken me through a few different worlds, but no matter what I've done, two themes have always been at the crux of it all: design and people.

Before UX

I was the creative lead for six years with an NGO and a local agency that promoted arts, crafts, and cultural heritage. I have been a part of these initiatives from research, inception, and actualisation, through to wrapping up. I loved being involved in the entire journey - understanding the people, listening to their stories, figuring out what was needed, and turning an idea into something tangible.

In my tenure with the NGO, I offered wraparound support to vulnerable women whose stories I heard and whose experiences shaped the way I approached my work. I helped tailor programs that offered them training and support that could lead to employment and financial independence - creating a more fulsome experience rather than simply addressing one need.

Those experiences taught me that the best work starts with people. It starts with listening before solving, understanding before creating, and being curious about the little things that can make someone's experience easier, better, or simply more meaningful.

Why UX

Perhaps that is why UX felt like such a natural next chapter for me. It brought together the things that had always been a part of who I am - my love for creating, my curiosity about people, and my instinct to make things better.

So here I am, foraying into the world of UX design and weaving a new story, adding another dimension to my journey, hoping to create designs that are fun yet meaningful, relatable yet purposeful.

Outside UX

When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me with a paintbrush in my hand. I paint murals and canvases, sketch whenever an idea strikes, and generally find any excuse to make something. It's my little bit of me-time - a space to slow down, get lost in color and creativity, and simply enjoy making something with my hands.

I'm still learning, still experimenting, and still playing with colors, ideas, and possibilities.

I hope my humble work strikes a chord with you, and that you have as much fun going through it as I had putting it together.

It is a piece of hard and heartwork.
